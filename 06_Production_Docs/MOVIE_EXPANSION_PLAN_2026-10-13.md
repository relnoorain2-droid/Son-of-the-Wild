# FROST: Son of the Wild - 100-130 Part Expansion Plan

Purpose: expand the existing 192-part Dola movie into a richer long-form cut without damaging the canon, the Hindi folders, the 30-second suspense hook rule, or the Dola safety rules.

## Target Length

- Current movie: 192 parts.
- Recommended expanded movie: 312 parts.
- Added parts: 120.
- Acceptable range: 292-322 parts if final pacing needs adjustment.

## Pacing Lock

Every new 30-second part must move the story forward. A part is allowed only if it does at least one of these:

- reveals new information,
- changes a character's decision,
- increases danger,
- deepens an important relationship,
- creates a new problem,
- pays off a previous setup,
- moves the characters to a new location,
- changes how the audience understands the villain, Lily, Bruno, Frost, or the kingdom.

Do not add a part only to repeat sadness, repeat fear, repeat travel, repeat training, or repeat village comedy. If a scene does not change the story, merge it into the previous or next part.

Each part should have this rhythm:

- 0-3s: immediate visual hook from the previous part.
- 3-20s: one clear new story beat with dialogue.
- 20-27s: turn, reveal, emotion, danger, or decision.
- 27-30s: suspense hook into the next part.

## Safety And Canon Rules For New Parts

- Lily and all brides must be adult women.
- No explicit wedding-night content, nudity, coercive bedroom visuals, or graphic harm.
- The prince can pretend romance and tenderness, but the scene must stay suspenseful, emotional, and safe.
- Use "bridal chamber", "private royal room", "tender lies", "fade away from intimacy", "before the wedding night can happen", and "Lily escapes before becoming his wife in truth."
- Show the cruel system through public ceremony, kingdom people, palace servants, guards, and frightened brides.
- The kingdom's citizens should be visible: market workers, castle servants, poor families, guards, nobles, priests, musicians, and common people watching the monthly selection.
- The final 3 seconds of every new normal part must include a suspense hook into the next part.
- Part 192's closure rule must move to the new final part after renumbering.

## Opening Expansion - Add 10 Parts Before Lily's Full Capture Arc

These should replace the fast version of current Part 001-003. The new opening should make the audience feel the whole system before Lily is chosen.

### New Opening Part A - "The Kingdom Watches"

Show Thorne Kingdom alive: workers, poor families, nobles, soldiers, servants, and fearful townspeople gathering for the prince's monthly bride selection. King Magnus sits above them like a judge. The people know the tradition but are too scared to speak.

Hook: a royal horn sounds and Captain Vex announces that the prince will choose.

### New Opening Part B - "The Bride Lottery"

Show eligible poor adult women standing with families in the palace square. Damian walks slowly through them, smiling like a gentleman while everyone trembles. He chooses the first bride publicly.

Hook: Damian touches the chosen bride's hand and says, "She will do."

### New Opening Part C - "Wedding Bells In A Silent City"

Show the public wedding with music, flowers, guards, priest, and forced celebration. The kingdom claps, but faces are scared. King Magnus watches coldly. The bride's mother cries silently.

Hook: the bride notices maids whispering behind the wedding curtain.

### New Opening Part D - "A Perfect Husband"

After the wedding, Damian acts sweet. He brings the bride to the royal dining hall, feeds her gently, calls her his queen, and makes her believe maybe the rumors are false. The audience should feel the manipulation.

Hook: Damian's smile disappears when the bride looks away.

### New Opening Part E - "The Bridal Chamber"

Night in the royal room. No explicit intimacy. Damian speaks softly, promises safety, and tells the bride to sleep early because "tomorrow begins our real life." She relaxes slightly, but a maid outside the door looks terrified.

Hook: the maid sees guards preparing a black carriage outside.

### New Opening Part F - "Before Sunrise"

Early morning. Damian wakes before the bride. He quietly dresses in dark riding clothes, hides his wedding ring, and orders guards to prepare the forest route. The bride wakes confused.

Hook: Damian says, "Come. I want to show you my favorite place."

### New Opening Part G - "The Forest Gate"

Damian takes the bride by carriage through the kingdom and into the forbidden forest road. He keeps pretending kindness. The bride sees claw marks, old torn veils, and frightened birds.

Hook: the carriage stops at the gate and the guards refuse to go farther.

### New Opening Part H - "No One Asks Questions"

At the forest gate, Damian's mask drops. He leaves the bride near the wild forest. No graphic harm. Use fog, animal shadows, distant growls, and the bride's terrified face.

Hook: the forest shadows move behind her.

### New Opening Part I - "The Kingdom Lies"

Morning in the kingdom. King Magnus announces the bride was unworthy, ran away, or was taken by fate. The citizens know it is a lie but stay silent. Rose the maid watches in horror.

Hook: Damian asks, "When is the next selection?"

### New Opening Part J - "The Next Name"

One month later, the kingdom gathers again. Captain Vex reads names. The selection system moves toward Lily Carter. Cut between the castle list and Lily helping Grace at home.

Hook: Vex's finger lands on "Lily Carter."

## Lily Suspense Expansion - Add 8-12 Parts Around Her Castle Escape

The current Lily wedding/escape should be expanded after the audience understands the monthly system.

- Lily is selected in front of the public, not only by a paper order.
- Grace begs for mercy while Lily tries to stay brave.
- Lily enters the kingdom and sees common people afraid to meet her eyes.
- The wedding is beautiful on the outside and terrifying underneath.
- Damian behaves lovingly in public so Lily is confused.
- In the private room, Damian pretends affection, praises Lily, and promises she will be safe.
- Lily notices clues: torn veil, hidden scratches on the door, Rose crying, a black carriage waiting before dawn.
- Rose finally whispers the truth: every bride is taken after the wedding.
- Lily realizes she must run that same night before any wedding-night intimacy happens.
- Lily escapes through servant halls, kitchen, laundry yard, and castle garden into the forest.

## Pacing Risk Check

The story will not be disturbed if the added parts stay inside the existing canon order:

1. Monthly bride system before Lily.
2. Lily selected, married, warned, and escaping before wedding-night intimacy.
3. Lily enters forest and meets Bruno.
4. Bruno and Lily trust grows into love.
5. Lily becomes pregnant.
6. Magnus attacks, Bruno dies saving Lily and unborn Frost.
7. Lily survives Cedar Hollow and Frost is born.
8. Frost grows fast because of Bruno's ancient Bigfoot genes and forest magic; his image changes cleanly from baby to kid to teen to adult while he becomes the village protector.
9. King returns, Frost learns truth, loses once, trains, receives Elder Orin power.
10. Frost wins the final war and the family/kingdom gets closure.

Main slow-down risks and fixes:

- Opening risk: too much kingdom ceremony. Fix: keep the first-bride arc to 10 parts, then move to Lily quickly.
- Lily castle risk: too much Damian pretending affection. Fix: every scene must add a clue that Lily is in danger.
- Bruno/Lily risk: too many sweet forest moments. Fix: alternate warmth with survival, trackers, food, shelter, fear, and trust decisions.
- Cedar Hollow risk: too many village-help scenes. Fix: each Frost help scene must change one villager's opinion or create a new problem.
- Training risk: repetitive power practice. Fix: each training part must unlock one power, reveal one family truth, or force Frost to choose gentleness over anger.
- Final war risk: action without story. Fix: each battle part must free someone, defeat a specific threat, or move Frost closer to Magnus.
