# Suspense Cliffhanger Pass - 2026-10-13

The 192-part Dola prompt package now includes an explicit suspense hook rule in every normal part.

## Rule Added To Parts 001-191

Each part must use the final 3 seconds as a hook into the next part. The hook can be:
- a worried look
- a mysterious sound
- a shadow
- a reveal
- an unfinished question
- danger approaching
- emotional doubt

The hook must follow the part's existing final frame and must not invent random new characters or break continuity.

## Final Part Rule

PART 192 uses a final emotional closure rule instead of a cliffhanger. It should feel complete, magical, and memorable.

## Why This Matters

The story already has suspense, but short-form audiences need a strong final beat in every 30-second part so they keep watching the next video.
