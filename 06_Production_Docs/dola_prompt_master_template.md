## Generic Visual Shot Template
Use for establishing or transition shots. Focus on location, mood, continuity, and camera movement.

## Talking Shot Template
Show the speaking character clearly, keep face visible, include exact dialogue timing and voice lock.

## Action Shot Template
Lead with a 0-3 second hook, clear movement, impact SFX, urgent music, and a final cliffhanger frame.

## Emotional Shot Template
Hold close on face/eyes, restrained body language, soft score, and a line that advances the story.

## Creature Reveal Shot Template
Begin with sound or shadow, reveal the creature clearly, show scale against environment, end on threat or awe.

## Cliffhanger Ending Shot Template
End at 22-30 seconds with a twist, question, reveal, danger, or emotional punch that points to the next part.
