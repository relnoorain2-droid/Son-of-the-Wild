# Style Rules

- Hyper-realistic cinematic 3D animation style.
- Unreal Engine 5 quality.
- 9:16 vertical image/video framing.
- Soft natural studio lighting for character references.
- Use approved character images as permanent identity references.
- No text, no logo, no watermark.
- Setting and people are fully American / Western, no Asian elements.
- Western fantasy kingdom only; no Asian palace styling.
- Pacific Northwest USA look: Washington/Oregon redwood and pine forests, log cabins, pickup trucks, rural American clothing.
- Dragons are Western dragons only: wings, fire, horns, bat-like wings.
- Haathi means woolly mammoth in this project.
- All dialogue must be natural American English.
- Lily always follows the Lily Look Lock: she remains visually 22-24 with the same face through the full movie.

## Text Overlay Rules

# Text Overlay Rules

Use on-screen text only when it helps the audience understand time, place, or a major chapter.

Allowed:
- Short location cards for 2-3 seconds.
- Short time jumps for 2-3 seconds.
- Short chapter/event cards when needed.

Examples:
- "MILLBROOK, OREGON"
- "THREE DAYS EARLIER"
- "CEDAR HOLLOW, OREGON"
- "NINE MONTHS LATER"
- "YEARS LATER"
- "THORNE CASTLE"

Rules:
- Text must be simple, cinematic, white or warm-gold, readable, and bottom or lower-third placed.
- Do not use subtitles for every dialogue line.
- Do not add random text, logos, watermarks, UI, misspelled labels, or extra captions.
- If the prompt asks for a place reveal, show the place name for only a few seconds, then remove it.

Important required card:
- When Lily reaches the poor rural village/cabin area, show "CEDAR HOLLOW, OREGON" for 2-3 seconds.
