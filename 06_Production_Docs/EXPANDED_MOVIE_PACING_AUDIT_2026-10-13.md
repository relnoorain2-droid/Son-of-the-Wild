## Story Order Check

The expanded story must keep this order:

1. First bride cycle shows the kingdom's monthly tradition.
2. Lily becomes the next selected bride.
3. Lily enters the castle, sees danger clues, and is warned by Rose.
4. Damian pretends love, but Lily escapes before wedding-night intimacy.
5. Lily enters the forest and Bruno saves her.
6. Bruno first offers to take Lily home.
7. Lily refuses because the king will find and kill her.
8. Bruno lets Lily stay because he understands she has nowhere safe.
9. Lily and Bruno's trust grows into love.
10. Lily becomes pregnant and the pregnancy is joy/hope.
11. Magnus finds them, Bruno dies saving Lily and unborn Frost.
12. Lily survives alone at Cedar Hollow.
13. Frost is born, his fast Bigfoot-gene growth is explained, his image changes baby -> kid -> teen -> adult, and he is accepted through repeated good deeds.
14. Frost learns truth only after Magnus harms Cedar Hollow.
15. Frost loses once, then gains ancestral power through Bruno and Elder Orin.
16. Frost defeats Magnus and the story ends with peace and Bruno's spirit.

No expansion should move Frost's truth reveal earlier, remove Bruno's sacrifice, or weaken Lily's survival arc.

## Frame-By-Frame Workability

For Dola 30-second parts, each new part should use this frame:

- 0-3s: visual hook from previous part.
- 3-8s: establish location/action immediately.
- 8-18s: dialogue and story movement.
- 18-24s: turn, clue, emotional shift, or danger.
- 24-27s: character decision or reveal.
- 27-30s: suspense hook into next part.

If a part cannot fit this frame, it should be merged or rewritten.

## Slowdown Watchlist

Avoid:

- multiple parts of only walking/traveling,
- multiple parts of only crying without new information,
- multiple parts of only villagers reacting the same way,
- repeated training without a new power or moral choice,
- repeated battle shots without changing the battle situation,
- too many sweet Bruno/Lily scenes without danger, survival, or trust progress.

Use instead:

- one new clue per castle scene,
- one new trust step per Bruno/Lily scene,
- one changed villager opinion per Frost village-help scene,
- one new power or family truth per training scene,
- one freed creature, defeated threat, or moral choice per war scene.
