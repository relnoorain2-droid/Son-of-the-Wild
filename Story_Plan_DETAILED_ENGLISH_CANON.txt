# FROST: Son of the Wild - Detailed English Canon Story

This is the corrected English master story. Use this as the story truth for all Dola prompts, character images, and later rewrites.

## Core Rule

The story is fully American / Western. The setting is Oregon / Pacific Northwest forests, rural American towns, log cabins, Western dark-fantasy castles, American people, Western dragons, grizzly bears, timber wolves, mammoths, dinosaurs, and mountain creatures.

No Asian architecture, no Hanuman-style Bigfoot, no Chinese dragon design. If a draft mentions "Chinese dragons," adapt it into huge Western flying dragons.

## Full Story From Start To End

Lily Carter is a poor young American woman living with her mother in a small Oregon town. They have very little money, very little food, and almost no protection. Deep inside the Pacific Northwest forest is a hidden kingdom ruled by King Magnus Thorne, a cruel forest king. His son, Prince Damian, follows a secret monthly tradition: he chooses a new bride, marries her, and after the ceremony secretly takes her toward the forbidden jungle. The public never hears the truth. The kingdom only says the brides disappeared, ran away, or were unworthy. In reality, Damian leaves them near the wild forest, where dangerous animals finish the job.

One month, Lily is chosen. King Magnus's soldiers come to her poor home and take her to the castle. Lily is frightened, but she tries to stay brave for her mother. The royal wedding happens. Before Damian can take Lily into the forbidden forest, an old maid named Rose warns her. Rose tells Lily that this has happened many times before: every month a bride enters the castle, and none of them return.

Lily realizes she has no safe future in the castle. Before Damian can carry out the old tradition, she escapes by herself into the jungle.

In the dark forest, wild animals surround Lily. Wolves close in, and a huge grizzly bear rises in front of her. At that moment, a massive black American Bigfoot appears from the darkness. His name is Bruno. He is terrifying in size, but his heart is gentle. Bruno saves Lily from the animals.

After saving her, Bruno kindly tells Lily, "Come. I will take you back home, where you came from. You came here by mistake."

Lily is terrified and refuses. Crying, she tells him that if she goes home, the king's people will find her and kill her. She says the king is searching for her, and if he sees her again, her life is over. She has nowhere else to go.

Bruno understands her helplessness. His heart softens. He gently tells her, "All right. You can stay here with me."

After this, Lily and Bruno begin living together in the forest. At first Lily fears him, but slowly she sees his kindness. Bruno protects her, feeds her, and shows her the hidden beauty of the jungle. They play together, swing on vines, run through the forest, laugh together, and slowly build an innocent and beautiful bond.

Over time, their bond becomes love. One night, they become husband and wife in spirit, shown only with tenderness, hand-holding, moonlight, and fade-to-black. Some weeks later, Lily realizes she is pregnant. Lily and Bruno are overjoyed. They dream about their coming child and imagine a peaceful life hidden away from the king.

One day, King Magnus enters the jungle with his army, giant savage beasts, dinosaurs, mammoths, and huge Western dragons. He is hunting and searching the forest. The king sees Lily and realizes she is the bride who escaped. Furious, he orders his soldiers and creatures to destroy everything and leave no witness.

Bruno sees Lily in danger and attacks the king's army without caring for his own life. He fights huge beasts, dark Bigfoots, soldiers, and dragons alone. The battle is terrible. Bruno is badly wounded, but he refuses to stop.

Bruno shouts to Lily, "Run! Save our child!"

Lily does not want to leave him, but Bruno uses his last strength to protect her. He pushes her safely into a fast river so the current can carry her away from the battlefield. Lily is swept away by the river. Behind her, King Magnus's army kills Bruno. Bruno dies protecting his wife and unborn child.

The river carries Lily far away. Her unconscious body reaches the bank near a poor rural American settlement called Cedar Hollow. When Lily wakes, she is near an old broken cabin. She is alone, pregnant, and heartbroken, but she forces herself to survive for the baby. She catches fish from the river, gathers food, repairs the cabin, and hides her pain.

The simple villagers ask where she came from and where her husband is. Lily hides the full truth and says only that her husband is dead. The villagers believe she is carrying a normal human child, and some of them begin helping her.

Months pass: six months, seven months, eight months, then nine months. One day, Lily realizes the baby is coming. She does not tell the villagers. Alone inside the old cabin, she gives birth.

The baby is born, and the great twist is revealed: Lily's child is a white baby Bigfoot. His name is Frost. He has snow-white fur, Lily's blue eyes, and Bruno's strength.

When the villagers see the white Bigfoot baby, they panic. Some say he will grow up and eat them. They tell Lily to leave the village. Lily begs them for a little time. She promises that her baby is innocent and asks them to let her stay for a few days. The villagers reluctantly agree.

As Frost grows, he begins helping the village. First in small ways, then in bigger ways. These helps must be shown across multiple scenes so the villagers slowly change from fear to love:

- Frost carries heavy logs alone so villagers can repair cabins before winter.
- Frost rescues a little girl from a broken bridge over a river.
- Frost pulls a stuck wagon and two horses out of deep mud.
- Frost catches fish from the river and leaves food outside poor families' doors.
- Frost protects children from wolves without hurting the children or villagers.
- Frost lifts a fallen tree off a farmer's roof after a storm.
- Frost carries water barrels during a barn fire and helps put out the flames.
- Frost saves a lost calf from a ravine and returns it gently to the farmer.
- Frost helps old Martha gather firewood and carries it to her porch.
- Frost blocks a charging bear and calmly leads it away from the village.
- Frost clears snow from the main road with his giant hands.
- Frost helps build a stronger bridge so the village can cross the river safely.
- Frost protects the village from bandits by scaring them away without graphic violence.
- Frost plays gently with children so everyone sees he has a kind heart.

Because of these repeated acts, the villagers slowly realize he is not a monster. He is their protector. When Lily finally prepares to leave Cedar Hollow, the villagers stop her and ask her and Frost to stay.

When Lily finally prepares to leave Cedar Hollow, the villagers stop her. They ask her and Frost to stay, because Frost has helped them more than anyone else ever has.

Years pass. Frost grows from a playful white Bigfoot child into a powerful young giant. He is kind like Bruno and brave like Lily.

One day, Lily and grown Frost go to the riverbank. While they are away, King Magnus and his soldiers pass through Cedar Hollow. The king's men attack the innocent villagers, collect taxes by force, and leave destruction behind.

When Lily and Frost return and see the ruined village, Lily breaks down crying. Frost asks her what happened and why this king is hurting their lives. For the first time, Lily tells Frost the full painful truth: Prince Damian's monthly bride secret, her escape, Bruno saving her, Bruno becoming her husband, and King Magnus's army killing Bruno.

When Frost hears that King Magnus killed his father and made his mother suffer, his anger explodes. He shouts that he will not spare the king.

Later, the king's soldiers return to Cedar Hollow to demand more taxes and money. Frost fights them alone. He throws aside soldiers and small dragons with ease. But King Magnus's forces include much larger and deadlier dragons and monsters. Frost is badly injured and nearly defeated.

After this, the villagers learn Bruno's name. An old man in the village, Old Joe, recognizes it. He explains that Bruno came from a powerful ancient bloodline. Bruno himself was gentle and did not carry the full magic, but Bruno's father, Elder Orin, was a legendary Bigfoot ancestor with great spiritual power.

Old Joe says that if Frost wants to defeat King Magnus's great army, he must seek the spirits of his father and ancestors and receive the ancient power of his bloodline.

Frost goes deep into the forest and meditates near his father's memory. He calls out to Bruno's spirit. The sky opens with magical light. Bruno's spirit appears, but Bruno does not release the power by himself. Instead, Bruno calls to his own father, Elder Orin. Elder Orin appears above Bruno as a vast ancient spirit.

Elder Orin releases the ancestral power. The power first strikes Bruno's spirit, then passes through Bruno into Frost. This shows that the power comes from the grandfather's ancient magic, through the father, into the son.

The magical energy enters Frost's body. Glowing tattoos form across his fur and skin. His eyes shine blue. His strength multiplies. He gains lightning power, flight, animal communication, and nearly unbreakable skin.

Now Frost, Lily, and the villagers prepare to attack King Magnus's kingdom. The king's own people are also tired of his cruelty, so many of them join Frost's side. Forest animals and freed creatures also join him.

King Magnus sends his full army: giant dinosaurs, savage dark Bigfoots, mammoths, monstrous beasts, and huge Western dragons flying through the sky. A massive final war begins.

During the battle, a huge dragon nearly defeats Frost. For a moment, Frost staggers. Then he remembers his mother Lily's suffering and his father Bruno's sacrifice. That memory unlocks his full divine power.

Frost rises into the air with lightning around him. He defeats the dragons, brings down the dinosaurs, shatters the king's army, and reaches King Magnus.

At the end, Frost defeats the cruel king and avenges Bruno. The kingdom is freed. Peace returns. Lily embraces her son, proud and heartbroken and finally safe.

The story ends with Lily, Frost, the villagers, the freed kingdom, and the forest creatures living in peace. Bruno's spirit watches from above, smiling. Lily knows that Bruno's love saved them all.

## Required Emotional Beats

- Bruno first offers to take Lily home.
- Lily refuses because she knows the king will find and kill her.
- Bruno lets Lily stay because he understands her helplessness.
- Lily and Bruno's love must grow gently over time through protection, play, trust, and kindness.
- Pregnancy must be shown as joy and hope, not shock or shame.
- Bruno must die saving Lily and the unborn child.
- Lily must survive alone in the rural village before Frost is born.
- Villagers must first fear baby Frost, then slowly accept him because he helps them.
- Frost must learn the truth only after the king harms Cedar Hollow.
- Elder Orin is the true source of ancient power. Bruno's spirit acts as the bridge between Elder Orin and Frost.
- The final battle must show Frost remembering Lily and Bruno before unlocking full power.

## Dola Safety Adaptation

The canon can be dark, but Dola prompts must phrase it safely.

Use:
- "the forest took them" instead of graphic death.
- "forbidden forest tradition" instead of explicit murder ritual.
- "escorted to the forest gate" instead of violent descriptions.
- "battle impact, silhouettes, slow motion" instead of gore.
- "tender fade-to-black" for Lily and Bruno's intimate relationship.
- "Western dragons" always, never Chinese dragons.

## Required On-Screen Place Cards

When Lily reaches the village/cabin area after the river carries her away, show this on screen for 2-3 seconds: "CEDAR HOLLOW, OREGON". Use similar short place/time cards whenever the audience needs clear context.


## Dialogue Production Rule

Every 30-second part must have strong voice. Use at least 3 spoken lines in each part, preferably 4-6 short lines. Character dialogue should carry emotion and story; narrator should only support context. No part should feel silent or boring. Dialogue must stay louder than music and sound effects.

## Opening Production Rule - Updated 2026-10-13

The generated movie must not begin with Lily immediately receiving the royal marriage order. The opening must first show the audience the kingdom system, the public bride selection, the fake wedding happiness, and the morning forest betrayal before Lily's turn begins.

The expanded opening should use multiple 30-second parts, not one rushed summary:

1. Show Thorne Kingdom alive with common people, poor families, palace servants, nobles, priests, musicians, guards, and frightened adult women gathered for the monthly bride selection.
2. Show Prince Damian choosing an adult bride publicly while King Magnus watches and the kingdom stays silent from fear.
3. Show the public wedding as beautiful on the outside but frightening underneath: music, flowers, clapping citizens, whispering maids, and the bride's family silently crying.
4. Show Damian pretending to be a loving husband after the wedding: gentle words, dinner, soft smiles, and false promises, so the bride and audience can feel the manipulation.
5. Show the bridal chamber only in a safe suspense style: no explicit intimacy, no nudity, no coercive bedroom visuals. Damian tells the bride to sleep early because tomorrow begins their real life.
6. Before sunrise, Damian wakes, prepares the black carriage, and takes the bride toward the forbidden forest while still pretending kindness.
7. At the forest gate, Damian's mask drops. He leaves the bride near the wild forest. Show fog, animal shadows, distant growls, and fear, but no graphic harm.
8. Show the kingdom lying afterward: the official story says the bride ran away, disappeared, or was unworthy. The people know something is wrong but cannot speak.
9. Then show the next monthly selection beginning and Lily Carter's name being chosen.

This structure is required so viewers understand that Lily is not the first bride. The king and prince repeat this same pattern every month, and Lily becomes the next victim of that system.

## Lily Castle Suspense Rule - Updated 2026-10-13

Lily's own castle arc must also breathe before she escapes.

- Lily should be selected in a public kingdom setting, with Grace begging and Lily trying to stay brave.
- Lily should enter the kingdom and see common people, servants, and maids afraid to meet her eyes.
- Damian should act loving in public so Lily becomes confused and unsure whether the rumors are true.
- In the private royal room, Damian can pretend affection and speak sweetly, but the scene must remain safe and suspenseful. Lily must escape before any wedding-night intimacy happens.
- Lily discovers clues: torn bridal veil, hidden scratches, Rose crying, guards preparing a black carriage before dawn, and whispered warnings about the missing brides.
- Rose tells Lily enough truth for Lily to understand that she must run that same night.
- Lily escapes through servant halls, kitchen corridors, laundry yard, castle garden, and then into the forest.
