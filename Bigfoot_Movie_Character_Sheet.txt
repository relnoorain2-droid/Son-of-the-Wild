Working title: "FROST: Son of the Wild"
Ratio: 9:16 | 312 parts x 30 sec | English dialogue | USA setting

==================================================================
## 1. HOW TO USE
==================================================================

Har character ki 2 images ChatGPT se banao:
  - FULL = poora body (head to toe)
  - FACE = chehra close-up

Steps:
1. Neeche diya STYLE LOCK har ChatGPT prompt ke shuru me paste karo.
2. Character ka prompt paste karo, image generate karo.
3. FACE ke liye usi chat me likho:
   Same character, same style, close-up portrait of face and shoulders, front facing, neutral grey background.
4. File ka naam bilkul table jaisa rakho, jaise:  C01_Lily_FULL.png  /  C01_Lily_FACE.png
5. Images isi folder me "Characters" sub-folder me rakho.

RULE: Ek character ki image final ho gayi to wahi hamesha use karni hai. Dobara mat banana, warna chehra badal jayega.

Naming code:
  C = main character | V = variant (same character, alag look) | S = supporting
  K = king's creatures / jungle animals | L = location

STYLE LOCK (copy-paste, har prompt se pehle):
----------------------------------------------------------------
Hyper-realistic cinematic 3D animation style, Unreal Engine 5 quality, photorealistic skin, hair and fur detail, soft natural studio lighting, sharp focus, vertical 9:16 image, full body visible head to toe, character standing straight facing the camera, arms relaxed, plain light-grey studio background, no text, no logo, no watermark.

==================================================================
Movie 100% American / Western rahegi. Koi Asian touch nahi.

- Jagah: Pacific Northwest USA (Washington/Oregon) – redwood/pine jungle, log cabins, pickup trucks, general store.
- Log: sab American naam, American chehre, American kapde (flannel, jeans, boots, cowboy hat).

- Dragons: sirf Western dragons (pankh, aag). Chinese/Asian dragon nahi.

- Jaanwar: grizzly bear, timber wolves, mountain lion, bison, elk, bald eagle.
- Power ritual: meditation/yoga/dhyan NAHI. Frost poore chaand ki raat ko apne baap ki kabr (grave) ke paas pathar pe ghutno ke bal baithkar baap ko pukarta hai – Western fantasy style.
- Shaadi: Western royal wedding (white gown, veil, church-style hall, candles).
- Music/song: American cinematic orchestra + soft country/folk song. Koi Asian instrument nahi.
- Dialogue: sirf natural American English.
- Har Dola prompt me yeh line: "Setting and people are fully American / Western, no Asian elements."

## PUNCH RULE (har 30 sec part me lock)
==================================================================
Koi bhi part "khaali" nahi hoga. Har part ka structure:

  0–3 sec  : HOOK – pehle 3 second me hi kuch chaunkane wala (action, cheekh, reveal, joke) taaki log scroll na karein
  3–22 sec : STORY – kahani aage badhe, kam se kam 1 dialogue line
  22–30 sec: PUNCH / CLIFFHANGER – end me jhatka, taaki log agla part dhoondhein

Har part me in 5 me se kam se kam 1 punch zaroor:
  1. ACTION   – fight, chase, dhamaka, jaanwar ka hamla
  2. FUN      – Frost/Bruno ki masti, bachchon ke saath comedy, funny reaction
  3. EMOTION  – aansu, pyaar, maa-beta moment
  4. TWIST    – naya raaz, naya dushman, achanak reveal
  5. WOW      – power, dragon, giant visual, slow-motion shot

Ek jaisa punch lagataar 3 part se zyada nahi (variety rahe).

STANDALONE RULE – har part akela bhi "best" lage:
Viewer kisi bhi part (Part 10, Part 87...) se shuru kar sakta hai. Isliye har part:
  - Bina pichhla part dekhe samajh aaye: pehli 1-2 lines me hi context mil jaaye
    (jaise: "They killed my father... now they're coming for my mother!")
  - Kam se kam 2-4 dialogue lines – koi bhi part bina voice ke nahi
  - Har part me teeno audio: VOICE (dialogue) + SFX (sound effects) + MUSIC (background score)
  - Har part me kam se kam 1 main character ka chehra clear dikhe
  - End me ek sawal/jhatka jo agla part dekhne pe majboor kare – aur viewer Part 1 se dekhne ka bhi soche
  - Har part apne aap me ek mini-story: hook -> kuch hota hai -> punch

Har Dola prompt me audio aise likha jayega (taaki koi voice miss na ho):
  DIALOGUE: [Character naam] ([voice lock]) says: "exact English line" – timing ke saath (jaise 4–7 sec)
  SFX: kaun si awaaz kab
  MUSIC: kis mood ka score
Har part ka aakhri frame agle part ka pehla frame banega (continuity).
